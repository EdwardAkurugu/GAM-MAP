The <b>GAM(M)-Based Malaria Analytics Platform (GAM(M)-MAP)</b> is an interactive R Shiny dashboard designed to analyse temporal and 
climatic drivers of uncomplicated malaria across Ghana’s former 
ten administrative regions. <b>Generalised Additive Models (GAMs)</b> form the basis of the analysis, while 
<b>Generalised Additive Mixed Models (GAMMs)</b> are used to incorporate 
correlation structures when diagnostics indicate residual autocorrelation.

<div style="color:green; ">
What This App Does?
</div>

This interactive tool analyses how climate variables influence malaria patterns across Ghana’s regions from 2012–2023.

<div style="color:green; margin-top:10px;">
Key Features:
</div>

- Regional GAM and GAMM modelling of climate–malaria relationships  
- Exploration of temporal patterns (trends, seasonality, time‑series decomposition)  
- Model choice guided by diagnostics: GAM when residuals are independent, GAMM with corARMA structures when autocorrelation is present  
- Performance evaluation and diagnostics for both GAM and GAMM fits  
- Evidence‑based insights to support malaria control strategies  

<div style="color:green; margin-top:10px;">
Data Coverage
</div>
12 years (2012-2023) | 10 Regions | Monthly observations

<div style="color:green; margin-top:10px;">
Alignment with National Goals
</div>
The app supports <b>Ghana’s National Malaria Elimination Programme (NMEP)</b> by providing climate‑informed analytical tools for understanding the temporal and climatic drivers of uncomplicated malaria across Ghana’s former ten administrative regions.
